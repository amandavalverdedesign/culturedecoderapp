Research Boundary

What the public record cannot prove.

Unknown

Employee experience

The audit can infer public brand culture and hiring expectations, but it cannot verify pace, psychological safety, compensation practices, or everyday autonomy without employee testimony or internal observation.

Unknown

Process maturity

Level 4 design maturity is supported by public outcomes. Level 5 would require proof that research systems, decision rights, and design authority are distributed beyond founder taste.

Caution

Customer love is not culture proof

Community advocacy supports brand reception, not internal culture. Enthusiast threads are included only as weak signals for language, ritual, and product experience.

Caution

Contradictions stay visible

The analysis keeps regulatory and scale-pressure counter-signals in view because they sharpen the mapping. Playful innovation is the advantage and the risk surface.
