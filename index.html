What is Rewarded, Tolerated & Punished

The organization protects its fantasy through unmistakable behavioral signals. These are the choices that earn a pool chair, receive a polite warning, or trigger immediate revocation of cabana privileges.

REWARDED
  • ⚡
    Aesthetic Obsession: Aligning designs to exact, pixel-perfect 1986 reference guidelines.
  • ⚡
    Atmospheric Depth: Enhancing narrative continuity with witty corporate brand assets.
  • ⚡
    Sensory Proof: Making fragrance, texture, sound, and color do measurable commercial work.
  • ⚡
    Committed Play: Treating the joke with enough rigor that customers can fully enter the world.
TOLERATED
  • ⏳
    Fidelity Delays: Slipping launch dates because vintage font kerning took hours.
  • ⏳
    Aesthetic Play: Crafting physical mockups of fictional corporate resort artifacts.
  • ⏳
    Useful Excess: Adding one more delightful detail when it strengthens recall and ritual.
  • ⏳
    Polished Debate: Challenging an idea vigorously, provided the critique arrives with a better poolside option.
PUNISHED
  • 🚨
    Modernist Slip: Deploying standard, sterile clinical UI blocks in design layouts.
  • 🚨
    Narrative Break: Expressing standard thinking like "It is only a cosmetic lotion company."
  • 🚨
    Generic Luxury: Replacing a specific cultural point of view with anonymous beige prestige.
  • 🚨
    Empty Spectacle: Shipping nostalgia that attracts attention but fails to clarify product value.
